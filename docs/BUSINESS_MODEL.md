# Business Model and Go-to-Market Plan

## Target segments

1.  Students and fresh graduates.
2.  Job seekers applying to multiple roles.
3.  Experienced professionals changing careers.
4.  Freelancers and contractors.
5.  Career coaches and education organizations in a later phase.

## Value proposition

A user can create a polished resume, improve its wording, compare it
with a specific job description, create a cover letter, and track the
application from one workspace.

## Pricing hypotheses

Treat these as experiments rather than final prices.

### Free

-   A small number of saved resumes.
-   Core templates.
-   Limited PDF exports or basic export access.
-   A limited number of AI actions.
-   Basic job tracking.

### Pro

-   More resume versions.
-   Premium templates and customization.
-   Higher AI usage limits.
-   Cover letter generation.
-   More detailed job-description analysis.
-   Expanded application tracking.

### B2B later

-   University and bootcamp accounts.
-   Career-coach workspaces.
-   Group onboarding and aggregate usage reporting that respects user
    privacy.

## Go-to-market experiments

-   Publish free resume templates and career-writing guides.
-   Partner with student societies and career centers.
-   Recruit a small beta group and interview users after their first
    export.
-   Measure resume completion and download rates.
-   Test a paid plan only after users repeatedly use features with
    ongoing value.
-   Offer referral incentives only after the core workflow is stable.

## Metrics

-   Landing-page-to-signup conversion.
-   Signup-to-first-resume completion.
-   First-resume-to-PDF-download conversion.
-   Time to first successful export.
-   AI suggestion acceptance rate.
-   Weekly active users.
-   Return rate over 7 and 30 days.
-   Paid conversion and churn.
-   Cost per active user and gross margin.

## Cost controls

-   Set AI quotas by plan.
-   Limit prompt and output size.
-   Monitor document processing and storage costs.
-   Avoid unnecessary repeated AI calls.
-   Use a queue for heavy jobs when needed.
-   Review pricing against actual provider costs.

## Positioning caution

Do not claim that an ATS score guarantees an interview or that the
product can guarantee employment. Market the feature as transparent
resume analysis and job-description alignment.
